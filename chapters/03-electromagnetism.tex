\section{Maxwell's Equations}

In vacuum, the differential form is
\begin{align}
  \grad\cdot\vect{E} &= \frac{\rho}{\varepsilon_0}, &
  \grad\cdot\vect{B} &= 0, \\
  \grad\times\vect{E} &= -\pdv{\vect{B}}{t}, &
  \grad\times\vect{B} &= \mu_0\vect{J}
    +\mu_0\varepsilon_0\pdv{\vect{E}}{t}.
\end{align}
