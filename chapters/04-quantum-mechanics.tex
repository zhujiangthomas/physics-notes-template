\section{States and Operators}

A state vector is denoted by $\ket{\psi}$. In the position representation,
\begin{equation}
  \psi(x,t)=\braket{x|\psi(t)}.
\end{equation}

Position and momentum obey the canonical commutation relation
\begin{equation}
  \comm{\hat{x}}{\hat{p}}=\ii\hbar.
\end{equation}

The time-dependent Schr\"odinger equation is
\begin{equation}
  \ii\hbar\pdv{}{t}\ket{\psi(t)}=\hat{H}\ket{\psi(t)}.
\end{equation}

For an early landmark in special relativity, see \cite{einstein1905}. A
standard modern introduction to quantum mechanics is \cite{griffiths2018}.
